# LSRW existing-Ours candidate oracle (post-hoc diagnostic, not a method row)

Source: the committed `ceiling_metrics_result.json`, 50 LSRW test images, frozen per-image PSNR/RGB-SSIM provenance. For each test image, select the highest-PSNR output among the six already-evaluated Ours candidates: `ours_ttt`, `ours_ttt_sdsd_knobs`, `ours_v2`, `ours_v2_sdsd_knobs`, `ours_ttt_target_tuned`, `ours_v2_ceiling`. Average that selected output's PSNR and RGB-SSIM across all 50 images. This **uses each test GT to choose the output per image** and is only an upper-bound diagnostic over these six fixed tensors, not a valid independent test row or a realizable target-free selection rule.

Result: oracle mean PSNR **16.9272 dB**, mean RGB-SSIM **0.5114**. Selection counts: `ours_ttt` 2, `ours_ttt_sdsd_knobs` 6, `ours_ttt_target_tuned` 2, `ours_v2` 4, `ours_v2_ceiling` 14, `ours_v2_sdsd_knobs` 22. For comparison, frozen MR. Illuminate+D is **17.8100 dB** mean PSNR and frozen QuadPrior+D is **0.5696** mean RGB-SSIM. Thus even a test-GT per-image oracle over the *existing* Ours outputs does not lead either metric on LSRW. This does **not** rule out a new, separately identified method or as-yet-unevaluated parameters; it rules out obtaining the requested lead merely by relabelling/selecting among these six existing outputs.

The 29-record LSRW target-tuning log gives best **raw Ours-TTT** mean PSNR 16.3053396947671 dB. Its declared tie rule selected a nearby 16.300686468354 dB setting; after D/κ selection, the reported Ours-v2 ceiling is 16.5388916030728 dB. The separate frozen Ours-v2 SDSD-knob transfer row is 16.5936865754451 dB. Thus the named ceiling is a finite-search result, not a mathematical upper bound even on the already evaluated Ours-v2 rows. Any expanded search must be disclosed as a new search and verified from its own outputs, not retroactively relabelled.
