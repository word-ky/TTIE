### Spatiality versus latent count

Uniform96 field variance is exactly **0** throughout. Spatial1 and global MSE are exactly equal on all 72 inputs. Max absolute MSE difference between uniform96 and global is **9.84582584e-6**, consistent with the explicitly documented raw-gradient/Adam-epsilon scaling effect; it is not extra spatial expressivity.

**H1: conditionally supported, not universal.** On midtone and high-key homogeneous illumination, spatial/global recoveries are nearly equal. On intrinsic dark structures, spatial4 is 19.408% worse under homogeneous darkening and 12.994% worse under homogeneous brightening. A uniform illumination does not prevent the prior from creating a harmful content-dependent correction field.

**H2: supported for resolvable, prior-compatible low-frequency shifts; fails as a blanket claim.** The midtone left/right, quadrant and gradient gains are large and persist across the noise seeds. Higher grid resolution helps those cases, but the four-cycle stripe gain is small and the twelve-cycle case reverses the ordering. Dark-content left/right spatial4 is 7.784% worse than uniform96, and dark-content spatial8 often deteriorates further. Eight-by-eight patch averaging/aliasing, field resolution, content prior, clipping and operator flexibility all contribute; this suite does not isolate a pure field-frequency causal effect.

**H3: supported only as the requested redundant-latent-count control.** Large low-frequency midtone improvements remain versus 96-scalar uniform96. Merely adding redundant optimizer scalars does not explain them. However, averaging sixteen vectors still yields only six effective output degrees of freedom, while a spatial grid has more. This control does **not** eliminate effective function-capacity as a confound or establish superiority over a richer globally uniform nonlinear ISP. No such stronger claim is made.

**H4: clearly supported.** The patch-mean prior changes valid content even while self-supervised loss falls: dark objects brighten, high-key images darken, and texture is suppressed. Even the favorable midtone clean input drifts. More spatial freedom can amplify the error (dark-structure spatial8). High-key smooth-gradient spatial4 improves versus uniform96 but remains worse than identity (0.0634104 versus 0.0478751 MSE), showing why comparison to identity must be retained.
